Overview

Designing around what people need to notice first.

How I approach promotional work

Marketing pieces often have a lot to say in a small space. I focus on making the main message easy to find first, then use typography, imagery, colour and spacing to guide the rest of the information without making the layout feel crowded.

Featured Project

Rangeen by Mirza

A colourful brochure with truck-art inspired details, bilingual content and a lot of information that still needed to feel organized.

The Direction

Let the personality stay loud without losing the information.

Rangeen by Mirza has a very expressive visual style, so the brochure needed to feel lively without becoming difficult to read. I used colour, pattern and illustration to carry the cultural character while grouping the content into clear sections. Repeated visual cues, strong section breaks and a consistent hierarchy help the pages stay connected even as the content changes.
