Overview

Designing for the product, not just the artboard.

How I approach packaging

A package can look great as a flat design and still fall apart once it is wrapped around a box, bottle or cup. I pay attention to the final format, how the branding will be seen, and how the information needs to sit on the actual product.

Featured Project

Kitchen de Brazil

One visual identity carried across bags, cups, trays, cans and boxes, with each format needing a slightly different approach.

Food Packaging System

One brand, several very different surfaces.

The main challenge was keeping the packaging connected without forcing the exact same layout onto every piece. A cup gives you a completely different space than a tray, box or bag, so I adjusted the scale and placement of the branding for each format while keeping the overall look consistent.

Design Approach

Letting each format do its job.

The illustrated toucan and supporting pattern help tie the system together. Instead of repeating one composition everywhere, I changed the amount of pattern, image scale and logo placement depending on the available space. That keeps each piece useful on its own while still making the full set feel related.
