Overview

How I approach identity work

I start with the personality of the business and where the identity will actually appear. Some projects need something restrained and professional, while others can be more playful or illustrative. The goal is not to make every logo look the same. It is to make each one feel right for the brand it belongs to.

Featured Project

Sip + Burn

An outdoor-inspired identity built around drinks, gathering and a warm cabin atmosphere.

The Idea

Bringing several parts of the brand into one mark.

Sip + Burn combines a cabin, trees, script lettering and smaller details connected to drinks and fire. The challenge was fitting those ideas together without letting the logo become too busy. I focused on keeping the main silhouette easy to recognize while using the smaller details to add personality.

Design Decisions

Warm, outdoorsy and detailed without feeling crowded.

The cabin and trees carry the outdoor feeling, while the script adds a more relaxed personality. I kept the supporting details small so they add meaning without competing with the name. The final mark feels illustrative, but still structured enough to work as a recognizable identity.

Colour Direction

A simple earthy palette that fits the mood.

Deep green and warm neutral tones support the outdoor character of the identity without competing with the illustration.
